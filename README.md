GHOSTLINE: Night Infiltration is a top-down 2D stealth game that runs entirely in the browser, no install and no network. You slip into a guarded facility after dark, lift every intel file, and reach the exit without ever being seen. Three facilities stand between you and a clean getaway: a warehouse, a server farm, and a rooftop lab wired with blinking laser grids.

Guards walk real patrol routes with raycast vision cones — walls and crates genuinely block their sight, so cover is honest. Get spotted and a guard's suspicion climbs: "?" means he is coming to look, "!" means he is chasing you down. Bushes slow his suspicion, sneaking keeps you quiet, and running is fast but loud enough to be heard. A thrown pebble rings out and pulls a nearby guard toward the noise, which is how you open a gap that was never there. Trip a lit laser and the alarm jumps; get caught three times and the night is over. Clear a level without ever being seen and the ghost bonus is yours.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Move with WASD or the arrow keys, hold Shift to run, hold C to sneak, press Q to throw a pebble, P to pause, M to mute.
